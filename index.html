Problem

The old workflow

A manual process that didn’t scale

Every new hire needed photo ID, TFN, licences, right-to-work, bank, super, contracts, police checks, and resume — all chased manually via email, spreadsheets, and paper forms.

10+ follow-ups per worker. 6–10 hrs of admin every week. The process created compliance risk and didn’t scale.

The breaking point

The moment that forced the issue was ISO accreditation. The existing manual process created real compliance risk. Document trails were inconsistent, follow-ups were hard to audit, and there was no centralised record of what had been collected.
